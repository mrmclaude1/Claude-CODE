## The one-paragraph thesis

Generic "start a newsletter / AI agency" advice ignores the Principal's single largest edge: **deep, credible, hard-to-fake domain authority in construction and capital projects.** A generalist AI developer cannot get a meeting with a general contractor's estimating chief; the Principal can. The portfolio is therefore built as a **barbell**: (1) a *concentrated, defensible operating business* in construction automation where the moat is domain trust, and (2) a *diversified, near-zero-labor passive engine* (index + dividend + crypto + real estate) that compounds regardless of the business. Profits from the operating business are pumped into the passive engine on a fixed waterfall until passive income covers living expenses — at which point active employment becomes optional.

## Honest framing (read this before believing any number)

- **Projections are scenarios, not promises.** Every financial figure in this repo is a modeled estimate with stated assumptions. Real outcomes depend on execution, market timing, and luck.
- **Income claims are uncertain by nature.** No business plan can guarantee revenue. Confidence scores (1–10) and downside cases are attached to every recommendation so you can size your bets accordingly.
- **Nothing here is personalized financial, legal, or tax advice.** The investing and acquisition sections are general education. Validate with a licensed advisor / CPA / attorney before committing capital or signing anything.
- **Templates are starting points, not executed legal documents.** The service-agreement template must be reviewed by a lawyer in your jurisdiction before use.

## What is already done vs. what needs you

**Done autonomously (in this repo):** strategy, scoring model, flagship business design, financial models, automation architecture, AI agent prompt library, SOPs, outreach/proposal templates, 90-day plan, risk register.

**Requires you (cannot be done autonomously):** anything touching money, identity, or external parties — registering an LLC, opening Stripe/bank/brokerage accounts, sending real outreach from your accounts, signing contracts, and making the final go/no-go call on the flagship. These are flagged throughout and consolidated in the 90-day plan.
