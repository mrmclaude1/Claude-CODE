It scores 6.99 on the scorecard and is the **highest-probability line on the board (90%)** — because it relies not on you out-executing competitors, but on the multi-century base rate of diversified markets compounding. Your only job is to automate contributions and not interfere.

## 2. The evidence base (why index-first)

- **Active management reliably loses over time.** Per S&P's SPIVA, **79% of active US large-cap funds underperformed the S&P 500 in 2025**, and over a 15-year horizon *no major category* had a majority of active managers beating their benchmark. ([SPIVA](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/spiva/article/spiva-us/))
- **Cost is the most reliable predictor of relative return.** 2025 average expense ratios: passive index funds **0.058%** vs. active **0.57%** — a ~0.3–0.5 percentage-point annual drag that compounds against you for decades. ([ICI](https://www.ici.org/news-release/mutual-fund-and-etf-fees-remained-near-historic-lows-in-2025), [Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/funds/active-versus-passive-etfs-why-lower-fees-still-win))
- **Conclusion:** a low-cost, broad-market index **core** is the default. Any "satellite" tilt must justify its existence against this baseline.

## 3. The allocation framework (core / satellite)

A simple, durable structure. Exact percentages are illustrative — calibrate to your age, risk tolerance, existing real-estate/crypto exposure, and a fiduciary's input.

```
                    PASSIVE ENGINE (illustrative)
   ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  CORE  (~80%)  — set-and-forget, broad, cheap              │
   │    • Total US market index            (e.g. VTI/FZROX)     │
   │    • Total international index         (e.g. VXUS)         │
   │    • Bonds / T-bills (age-appropriate) (e.g. BND/SGOV)     │
   ├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
   │  SATELLITE (~20%) — deliberate, bounded tilts             │
   │    • Dividend-growth sleeve            (e.g. SCHD/NOBL)    │
   │    • Crypto core: BTC/ETH only         (1–5% of TOTAL)    │
   └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Core (~80%) — the engine block
Total-market US + international index funds, plus an age-appropriate bond/T-bill allocation. This is the bulk of the money and it never gets touched except for rebalancing. Minimize fees; that's the whole edge.

### Satellite (~20%) — bounded, intentional tilts
- **Dividend-growth sleeve.** Quality tilt, lower volatility, a growing income stream. Note the trade-offs honestly: the S&P 500's broad yield is only **~1.07%** (June 2026, near historic lows), Dividend-Aristocrat ETFs like NOBL yield **~2.55%** (0.35% ER), and "aristocrat" status does **not** guarantee against dividend cuts and has lagged the tech-heavy index recently. Use it for *income stability and behavior*, not as a return-maximizer. ([Sure Dividend](https://www.suredividend.com/dividend-aristocrats-list/), [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/economic_indicators/150/sp-500-dividend-yield))
- **Crypto core.** Mainstream institutional consensus now sizes crypto at **1–5% of total portfolio**, BTC-centric, *not* alts: BlackRock calls 1–2% bitcoin "reasonable" (risk comparable to a single Magnificent-7 stock); Fidelity suggests 2–5% (up to ~7.5% for long-horizon investors). **Critical note for you:** you already hold crypto and real estate — these bands are *total-portfolio*, so your existing exposure may already fill or exceed the sleeve. Measure before adding. ([BlackRock](https://www.blackrock.com/ch/professionals/en/insights/portfolio-design/sizing-bitcoin-in-portfolios), [asset-manager survey](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/major-asset-managers-propose-1-to-5-crypto-portfolio-allocation/))

## 4. Account-funding order (US, general best practice)

The *order* you fill accounts matters as much as what you buy, because tax drag compounds. The widely-cited sequence (precede everything with an emergency fund + paying off high-interest debt):

1. **401(k) up to the full employer match** — an instant 50–100% return; never leave it on the table.
2. **Max the HSA** (if on an HDHP) — the only triple-tax-advantaged account: deductible in, tax-free growth, tax-free out for medical.
3. **Max a Roth IRA** — tax-free growth and withdrawals; ideal for a long horizon.
4. **Finish maxing the 401(k).**
5. **Taxable brokerage** — unlimited, flexible, where waterfall overflow lands.

([Bogleheads](https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Prioritizing_investments), [Berger](https://robberger.com/the-optimal-order-of-funding-retirement-accounts/)) — *order flexes with your specifics; confirm with a CPA.*

## 5. The automation (this is the "engine" part)

The entire point is that this runs without you. Set it up once:

| Step | Action | Frequency | Your time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Automatic transfer** from operating/checking → brokerage on a fixed date | Monthly | 0 (set once) |
| 2 | **Auto-invest** the deposit into target funds (most brokerages support recurring buys) | Monthly | 0 (set once) |
| 3 | **Rebalance** back to target weights when any sleeve drifts >5 absolute points | ~Quarterly check | 10 min |
| 4 | **Tax-loss harvest** opportunistically in the taxable account | Annual | 30 min (or robo-automated) |
| 5 | **Review** allocation vs. life changes | Annual | 1 hr |

Dollar-cost averaging via fixed recurring buys removes timing decisions (and timing mistakes). The Friday "Improve & Invest" block in your weekly rhythm is when you confirm the monthly buy fired — that's the entire ongoing workload.
