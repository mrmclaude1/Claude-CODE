## The four red risks — your standing priorities

1. **F1 — validate before building.** The entire Rung-1 design exists to neutralize this. Do not skip it.
2. **F2 — document liability.** Human QA + legal review + E&O are non-negotiable before any real document ships.
3. **W1/W3 — behavioral & tax discipline.** Separate accounts and automation remove the moments where you could make the fatal mistake.
4. **S1 — focus.** One business at a time. The portfolio is built *sequentially*, funded by the waterfall — not by doing everything at once.

**Confidence in this register: 7/10** — it covers the known risks; the unknown ones get caught by the improvement loop's monthly review and added here.
