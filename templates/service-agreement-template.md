## SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] between:

**Provider:** [Your business name / entity], [state] [entity type] ("Provider")

### 1. Services
Provider will prepare draft payment-application packages (including AIA-style G702/G703 continuation sheets and schedules of values) and draft lien-waiver documents for Client's designated projects ("Services"), as further described in Exhibit A.

### 2. Client responsibilities
Client will provide accurate and timely contracts, schedules of values, progress information, and project data. **Client is solely responsible for reviewing, verifying, executing, and submitting all documents.** Provider's outputs are drafts prepared for Client's review.

### 3. No legal or accounting advice
Provider is not a law firm or accounting firm and does not provide legal or accounting advice. Documents prepared under this Agreement are administrative work product. **Client is responsible for obtaining legal review of lien waivers and payment documents as Client deems necessary.** [Attorney to expand.]

### 4. Review and approval
All deliverables are provided as drafts marked for Client review. Provider has no authority to submit documents on Client's behalf unless expressly agreed in writing. Client's submission of any document constitutes Client's acceptance of its contents.
