# Capital Allocation Waterfall

How every dollar of profit gets routed. This is the family-office "treasury policy." It's dynamic —
the percentages shift as the portfolio matures through three stages. Run it monthly (Friday block).

## Core principle
Money flows from **labor-like income → automated income → liquid compounding assets → financial
independence.** Early on, reinvest aggressively into the engine. Later, shift toward durable,
passive assets.

```
Business Profit
   │
   ├─► 1. Taxes reserve (set aside FIRST, always)
   ├─► 2. Operating buffer (keep runway topped to 6 months)
   ├─► 3. Reinvest into the engine (automation, VA, SaaS build, marketing)
   ├─► 4. Capital portfolio (Build 3 — index/dividend/crypto)
   ├─► 5. Real estate fund (down-payment accumulation)
   └─► 6. Acquisition war chest (buy cash-flowing assets later)
```

## Stage-based allocation (of profit *after* tax reserve + buffer)

### Stage 1 — Ignition (Year 1, MRR < $5k): *reinvest to grow*
| Bucket | % | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Reinvest in engine (automation/VA/SaaS/marketing) | 50% | Highest ROI is growing the cash machine itself. |
| Capital portfolio | 30% | Build the habit + tax-advantaged space early (compounding loves time). |
| Real estate fund | 15% | Accumulate toward the next property. |
| Acquisition war chest | 5% | Start the fund; opportunistic. |

### Stage 2 — Traction (MRR $5k–$20k): *balance growth and durability*
| Bucket | % |
|---|---:|
| Reinvest in engine | 30% |
| Capital portfolio | 35% |
| Real estate fund | 20% |
| Acquisition war chest | 15% |

### Stage 3 — Compounding (MRR > $20k or stable): *shift to durable assets*
| Bucket | % |
|---|---:|
| Reinvest in engine | 15% |
| Capital portfolio | 35% |
| Real estate | 25% |
| Acquisitions | 25% |

## Hard rules (the guardrails)
1. **Taxes come out first.** Reserve ~30–40% of profit (confirm rate with your CPA). Never spend it.
2. **Buffer before growth.** Maintain 6 months of combined business + personal runway in cash before
   funding buckets 4–6.
3. **Tax-advantaged space first** inside the capital portfolio: Roth IRA + **Solo 401(k)** (a major
   lever for self-employed profit — talk to a CPA in Year 1).
4. **No bucket-jumping on emotion.** Stage shifts happen at the MRR thresholds, reviewed quarterly.
5. **Crypto stays ≤ 5%** of the capital portfolio (IPS rule), regardless of how good it feels.
6. **Acquisitions must cash-flow on day one** and pass the same scorecard discipline as a build.

## The end state (why this works)
Income builds automation → automation builds more businesses → businesses generate cash flow →
cash flow buys real estate and acquisitions → those feed the investment portfolio → the portfolio's
safe-withdrawal income covers your life. At that point **employment is optional** — which is the
entire objective.

## Worked example (one month, Stage 1)
- Service net profit: **$7,000**
- Tax reserve (35%): **−$2,450** → set aside
- Buffer top-up (if needed): assume full → $0
- Distributable: **$4,550**
  - Reinvest engine (50%): **$2,275** (VA hours, a new automation, a bit of outreach tooling)
  - Capital portfolio (30%): **$1,365** (auto-buy into the IPS allocation)
  - Real estate fund (15%): **$683**
  - Acquisition war chest (5%): **$227**
