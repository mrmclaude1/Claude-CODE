# Scoring Methodology

The Overall Score (0–100) is a weighted composite designed for **your specific situation**:
capital under $500, 1–10 hrs/week, deep construction/PM/automation expertise, and a goal of
*building assets* (not just income). Weights reflect that bias.

## Weighted Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Why this weight |
|---|---:|---|
| Competitive Advantage (your moat) | 18% | This is the single biggest predictor of success for a solo operator. Your construction/PM domain knowledge is a rare, hard-to-copy edge. Generic ideas score low here on purpose. |
| Probability of Success | 16% | An 80%-likely $4k/mo beats a 20%-likely $20k/mo on a risk-adjusted basis. |
| Automation Potential | 14% | Directly serves the "minimize my time" constraint. |
| Long-Term Asset Value | 12% | You want assets that compound, not a job. |
| Scalability | 10% | Ceiling on the outcome. |
| Expected Income (12-mo run-rate) | 10% | Cash flow funds the rest of the waterfall. |
| AI Advantage | 8% | How much leverage AI gives *you specifically* vs. competitors. |
| Risk (inverted) | 7% | Capital preservation; legal/regulatory exposure. |
| Startup Cost + Payback (inverted) | 5% | You're capital-constrained, so this matters but is not decisive. |

## How to read each column

- **Startup Cost** — out-of-pocket to launch (you already own compute, internet, AI subs, server).
- **Monthly Time** — steady-state hours/week × 4 after the build phase, not the launch crunch.
- **Automation Potential (1–10)** — share of recurring work that AI/n8n/scripts can carry.
- **Difficulty (1–10)** — execution complexity for *you* given your skills (lower is easier).
- **Risk (1–10)** — probability-weighted downside, incl. legal/regulatory (lower is safer).
- **Scalability (1–10)** — revenue ceiling without proportional time.
- **Expected Monthly Income (mo 12)** — realistic run-rate by month 12 *if executed*, not a peak.
- **Probability of Success (%)** — odds of reaching a self-sustaining state within 12 months.
- **Payback (months)** — time to recover startup cost from profit.
- **Long-Term Asset Value (1–10)** — does it become more valuable every month (IP, audience, MRR, data)?
- **Exit Value** — realistic sale basis. Service ≈ 2–5× SDE; SaaS ≈ 3.9–5× SDE / 2.5–6× ARR (2025 data).
- **Competitive Advantage (1–10)** — your durable edge in this specific market.
- **AI Advantage (1–10)** — how much AI multiplies your output here.

## Honesty notes (read these)

1. **The numbers are estimates, not promises.** Income figures assume consistent execution at the
   stated time budget. The biggest variable is *you actually doing the outreach* in months 1–3.
2. **Probability of success is calibrated, not optimistic.** Even the #1 pick is 70% — meaning a
   ~3-in-10 chance it stalls. That's why we run a *portfolio*, not a single bet.
3. **Ranking is for your profile.** A generic person should not copy this. The construction plays
   rank #1–#2 *because of your background*; for someone without it they'd rank far lower.
4. **The bottom of the list (ranks 18–22) is included deliberately** so you can see what was
   rejected and why — dropshipping, day trading, generic content farms. These are popular but
   score poorly on competitive advantage and probability of success. We do not pursue them.
