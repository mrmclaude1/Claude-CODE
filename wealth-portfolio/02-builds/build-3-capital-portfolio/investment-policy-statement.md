# Build 3 — Investment Policy Statement (IPS)

This is the boring engine that actually delivers "employment becomes optional." Business income is
the *fuel*; this portfolio is the *destination*. A written IPS exists so that future-you doesn't
make emotional decisions in a crash. **Read this before acting in any market panic.**

> ⚠️ Educational, not personalized financial advice. Confirm with a fiduciary advisor and your own
> tax situation. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy/sell a specific security.

---

## 1. Objective
Convert volatile, illiquid business income into a diversified, liquid, compounding asset base
sufficient to make active employment optional. Target: a portfolio whose safe-withdrawal income
(~3.5–4%/yr) eventually covers living expenses.

## 2. Philosophy (evidence-based, low-cost, boring on purpose)
- **You will not try to beat the market.** Your edge is in your businesses, not in stock-picking.
- **Low cost compounds.** Expense ratios of 0.03–0.06% vs. 1%+ active funds = enormous over decades.
- **Diversified + automated > clever + manual.** Time in market, not timing the market.
- Historical baselines used for planning (NOT guarantees): broad US equity ~10%/yr nominal long
  run; VOO ~15.6%/yr last decade, SCHD ~12.6%/yr + ~3.5% yield (stockanalysis, Motley Fool 2026).
  **Plan with ~6–7% real to stay conservative.**

## 3. Target Allocation (the "core + sleeves" model)

| Sleeve | Target % | Vehicle examples | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Core equity (growth)** | 55% | Total US market (VTI) / S&P 500 (VOO) + total international (VXUS) | Long-run compounding |
| **Dividend / income** | 20% | Schwab US Dividend (SCHD) or similar | Cash flow + downside cushion |
| **Bonds / stability** | 10% | Total bond (BND) / short-term treasuries | Ballast, dry powder |
| **Crypto (high-risk sleeve)** | 5% | BTC + ETH only (blue-chip), held in cold storage | Asymmetric upside, capped downside |
| **Cash reserve** | 10% | HYSA / money market | 6-mo business+personal runway |

Rationale for splits:
- The 55/20 equity/dividend mix follows the well-supported 70–80% growth / 20–30% income guidance
  (Bogleheads-style), tuned toward growth given your long horizon and separate dividend cushion.
- **Crypto capped at 5%.** You have crypto experience, but position-sizing is the discipline that
  separates investing from gambling. 5% can 10× without threatening the plan; if it goes to zero,
  the plan survives. Avoid altcoin speculation and leverage entirely.
- **Cash reserve is deliberately high (10%)** because your income is lumpy/self-employed.

## 4. Contribution Policy (this is where the waterfall connects)
- Automate contributions: a fixed % of every business profit distribution flows here (see
  `05-finance/capital-allocation-waterfall.md`).
- **Dollar-cost average** on a schedule (e.g., monthly auto-buy). No market timing.
- Max out tax-advantaged space first: Roth IRA, and a **Solo 401(k)** once self-employed income
  exists (huge contribution limits for business owners — a major, legitimate tax lever).

## 5. Rebalancing Policy
- Review quarterly (calendar reminder; see operations). Rebalance only when a sleeve drifts
  **>5 percentage points** from target, or annually — whichever comes first.
- Rebalance with new contributions first (tax-efficient) before selling.

## 6. Risk & Behavior Rules (the most important section)
- **No selling in a downturn.** Drawdowns of 20–50% are normal and expected. The plan assumes them.
- No leverage. No options income schemes. No "hot tip" concentration.
- No single non-fund position > 5% (except diversified index funds).
- During a crash: do nothing except continue scheduled buys. Re-read §6.

## 7. Tax Efficiency
- Hold tax-inefficient assets (bonds, REITs) in tax-advantaged accounts.
- Harvest losses where appropriate. Hold > 1 year for long-term cap-gains treatment.
- **Solo 401(k) for the business** — coordinate with a CPA; this can shelter a large share of
  business profit and is one of the highest-ROI moves available to a profitable solo operator.

## 8. Review Cadence
- Quarterly: rebalance check + contribution review.
- Annually: re-read this IPS, update targets only with a written rationale (not emotion).
