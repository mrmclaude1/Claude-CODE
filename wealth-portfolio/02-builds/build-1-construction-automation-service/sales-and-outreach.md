# Sales & Outreach Playbook — Build 1

The single highest-leverage activity in this entire portfolio is **2 focused hours of outreach per
week for the first 90 days.** Everything else is built. This is the part only you can do (at first).

---

## The 90-day plan

| Weeks | Goal | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Build the list + assets | 50-name list, landing page, 2 case-study "stories" (even hypothetical "here's what I'd build"), pick a name + domain. |
| 3–6 | First conversations | 10 personal outreaches/week → book 3–5 discovery calls/week. |
| 7–10 | First closes | Convert calls to 1–2 paying setups. Deliver fast. Capture a real result. |
| 11–12 | Referral flywheel | Turn first results into case studies + 2 referral intros each. |

## The 50-name list (build this first)

Categories to mine from your career:
- Trade contractors you've worked with (subs on your capital projects)
- GCs and their PMs/estimators
- Vendors and suppliers (they know every contractor in the area)
- Office managers / owners' spouses who run the back office
- Anyone who's ever complained to you about "paperwork" or "the office side"

Put them in the CRM (Airtable/Baserow). Fields: Name, Company, Trade, Revenue est., Relationship,
Known pain, Last contact, Status.

## Outreach templates

### A. Warm intro (someone you know) — email/text

> Hey [Name] — quick one. I've been building automation systems that take the back-office
> paperwork off contractors' plates — bid follow-ups, RFIs/submittals, chasing overdue invoices,
> COI tracking. Basically the stuff that eats nights and weekends. Given [specific thing you know
> about their business], thought of you. Worth a 15-min call to see if it'd save you real time? No
> pitch — if it's not a fit I'll tell you.

### B. Warm referral ask (after a good conversation/result)

> Glad that's working for you. Who are two other [trade] owners you respect who are drowning in the
> office side? I'll set them up with the same thing and send you a [referral reward] when one comes
> on board.

### C. Cold-but-credible (LinkedIn/email, use only with real specificity)

> [Name] — I spent years running capital projects (budgets, schedules, the whole machine) and now
> I build automation for contractors. I just set up a system for a [size] [trade] contractor that
> [specific result, e.g. "recovered ~$6k/mo in quotes that were going cold"]. If chasing
> quotes / submittals / overdue invoices is a headache at [Company], I can probably automate it.
> Open to a 15-min teardown of one workflow — free, no pitch?

### D. Supply-house / association partnership

> I run automation for contractors and I'd love to do a free lunch-and-learn for your
> members/customers on "killing the 5 most time-wasting paperwork tasks in a contracting business."
> No selling — pure value. Builds goodwill for [supplier] and gets your customers a useful session.

## Discovery call structure (15–20 min) — you're a PM, run it like a scope meeting

1. **Their world (5 min):** "Walk me through what happens from when a job request comes in to when
   you get paid. Where does it get stuck?" — *Listen for the most-repeated complaint.*
2. **Quantify the pain (3 min):** "How many hours/week does that eat? What's it cost you when it
   goes wrong (lost bid, late invoice)?" — *This becomes your value-based price anchor.*
3. **Show the path (3 min):** "Here's how I'd automate that specific thing." One workflow, concrete.
4. **The offer (2 min):** Recommend ONE tier. Setup + retainer. Fixed price. (Don't over-offer.)
5. **Close (2 min):** "I can have the first version live in [X days]. Want me to send the
   agreement?" — Send the SOW same day (template in `templates/`).

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | Reframe to value: "It recovers ~$[X]/mo we just identified — it pays for itself in [Y] weeks." Offer the Starter tier as an entry. |
| "We already use [Procore/QuickBooks/etc.]" | "Great — I automate *around and between* your tools. I'm not replacing them; I'm connecting them so the paperwork stops falling through the cracks." |
| "I don't trust AI with my business" | "Fair. Anything compliance-critical keeps a human checkpoint. AI drafts; a person approves. You stay in control." |
| "Let me think about it" | "Totally. What specifically do you want to think through? Often I can answer it now." Then book a firm follow-up date (PM discipline). |
| "Can you just do it once for cheaper?" | "I could, but it'll break when your process changes — that's why dissatisfaction with one-off automation is so high. The retainer keeps it working as you grow." |

## The referral flywheel (this is what makes it scale without ad spend)

1. Deliver a result you can quantify.
2. Capture it: one-paragraph case study + a number (use the SOP).
3. Ask for 2 intros (template B).
4. Reward referrals (a free tune-up or a month off).
5. Post the case study (template C content) → inbound starts.

Target: **every client produces ≥1 new client.** At that ratio, the business compounds.
