# SERVICE AGREEMENT & STATEMENT OF WORK (TEMPLATE)

> ⚠️ **Not legal advice.** This is a practical starting template. Have a lawyer review before first
> use, and confirm your business structure (an LLC is strongly recommended before signing clients —
> see the legal/setup checklist at the bottom). Fill every `[BRACKET]`.

---

**This Agreement** is between **[YOUR LLC NAME]** ("Provider") and **[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]**
("Client"), effective **[DATE]**.

## 1. Services
Provider will design, build, and maintain automation workflows as described in the Statement of
Work (Exhibit A). Provider provides **business-process automation and software-integration
services only**. Provider is **not** acting as a licensed contractor, engineer, architect, or
accountant, and provides no professional engineering, legal, or accounting advice.

## 2. Statement of Work (Exhibit A)
- **Workflow(s) in scope:** [e.g., "Bid follow-up automation" + "AR collections reminders"]
- **Specific deliverables:** [bullet the exact outputs]
- **Out of scope:** anything not listed above (handled via change order, §7).
- **Launch target date:** [DATE]
- **Revisions included:** [N] rounds during setup.

## 3. Fees & Payment
- **Setup fee:** $[AMOUNT], due in full before work begins.
- **Monthly retainer:** $[AMOUNT]/month, billed on the [Nth] via Stripe, covering monitoring,
  maintenance, one improvement/month, and support per §4.
- **Term:** Month-to-month after setup. Either party may cancel with **30 days'** written notice.
- Late retainer payments may pause service after [N] days.

## 4. Support & SLA
- Support channel: [email/Slack]. Response within **[1 business day]**.
- **Critical errors** (a live workflow fails): best-effort response within **[same business day]**.
- Provider monitors workflows and will notify Client of detected failures.

## 5. Human-in-the-Loop / Limitations
Client acknowledges automations may require human review, especially for **compliance- or
money-critical steps** (e.g., insurance/COI expirations, invoicing, legal notices). Provider will
implement reasonable human-approval checkpoints for such steps. **Client remains responsible** for
final review of compliance- and finance-critical outputs.

## 6. Client Responsibilities
- Provide timely access, accurate data, and a point of contact.
- Maintain its own licenses/subscriptions for third-party tools.
- Review and approve outputs where checkpoints are specified.

## 7. Change Orders
Work outside Exhibit A requires a written change order specifying scope, price, and timeline,
approved by both parties (email approval suffices) before work begins.

## 8. Intellectual Property
- **Client owns** its data and the configured workflows specific to its business upon full payment.
- **Provider retains** ownership of its underlying templates, frameworks, reusable components, and
  know-how, and may reuse them for other clients. *(This clause is what lets the service feed
  Build 2 — keep it.)*

## 9. Confidentiality
Each party will protect the other's confidential information and use it only to perform this
Agreement.

## 10. Data & Security
Provider will use reasonable measures to protect Client data. Client grants Provider access solely
to provide the Services. On termination, Provider will return or delete Client data on request
(§12).

## 11. Warranties & Limitation of Liability
Services are provided "as is" with reasonable care. **Provider's total liability under this
Agreement is limited to the fees paid by Client in the prior [3] months.** Neither party is liable
for indirect or consequential damages. *(Critical — keep a liability cap.)*

## 12. Termination
Either party may terminate per §3. On termination, Provider will (a) stop work, (b) provide a data
export, and (c) per Client's election, transfer or decommission workflows. Earned fees are
non-refundable.

## 13. Independent Contractor
Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of Client.

## 14. Governing Law / Disputes
Governed by the laws of **[YOUR STATE]**. Disputes resolved by [mediation then arbitration in YOUR
COUNTY] / [the courts of YOUR COUNTY].

## 15. Entire Agreement
This Agreement (with Exhibit A) is the entire agreement and supersedes prior discussions. Changes
must be in writing.

## Legal / Setup checklist (do before signing client #1)
- [ ] Form an **LLC** in your state (~$50–$300; the only likely-justified spend above the $500 cap —
      it caps personal liability and is table stakes for B2B).
- [ ] Get an **EIN** (free, IRS).
- [ ] Open a **business bank account** + Stripe.
- [ ] Consider **general liability + tech E&O insurance** once revenue justifies (~$50/mo) — many
      contractor clients will ask for a COI.
- [ ] This template reviewed by a local attorney once (reuse forever after).
- [ ] Privacy basics: a simple privacy policy on the site; a data-handling note for clients.
