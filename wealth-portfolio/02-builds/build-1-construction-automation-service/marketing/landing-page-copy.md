## The problem (mirror their world)
You didn't get into [electrical / mechanical / concrete / roofing] to babysit a spreadsheet.
But right now:
- Quotes go out and **nobody follows up** — jobs you already won on merit die in silence.
- Overdue invoices pile up because **chasing them is everyone's job and no one's job.**
- RFIs, submittals, COIs, change orders live in **email, texts, and memory** — until one gets missed.
- The office person is **drowning**, and if they quit, it all falls on you.

## The fix (what I actually do)
I connect the tools you already use and automate the repetitive back-office work — the stuff that
eats time and causes expensive mistakes. You keep control; AI does the typing and the chasing.

- **Bid Follow-Up Autopilot** — every quote gets a smart follow-up sequence; hot replies hit your phone.
- **Collections Chaser** — overdue invoices get polite, escalating reminders automatically.
- **Submittal / RFI / Change-Order Tracker** — nothing slips; everything has a status and a deadline.
- **COI & Sub Onboarding** — insurance expirations tracked and flagged before they bite you.

## Why me (the unfair part)
I ran budgets, schedules, and crews on capital projects for years. **I speak your language** — I'm
not a tech bro who's never read a set of plans. I know the difference between a submittal and a
change order, and I know what it costs you when one gets missed. I build the system *and* keep it
working as your business changes.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. **15-min call** — you show me where it hurts; I tell you straight if I can help.
2. **I build it** — one painful workflow automated and live, usually within a week or two.
3. **It runs** — I monitor and tune it monthly; you get the time back.
