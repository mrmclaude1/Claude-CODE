## The one-paragraph thesis
Everyone has AI now, so "I use AI" is not a moat. **Your moat is that you're a construction /
capital-projects manager who can *also* build automation** — a rare overlap. We exploit it by
building a 3-asset engine: (1) a **productized AI-automation service for contractors** for fast cash
flow, (2) a **construction-niche micro-SaaS** spun from the service's proven patterns for a sellable
asset, and (3) a **boring, automated capital portfolio** where the profits compound into freedom.
Service funds SaaS; both fund the portfolio; the portfolio funds your life.

## Why this beats the alternatives
We rejected day trading, dropshipping, generic content farms, and "generic AI agency" — all popular,
all low on competitive advantage and probability of success for your profile (see
`01-portfolio/ranking-rationale.md`). The construction angle wins because it's where your
credibility is uncopyable and the market is underserved and paying.

## The money math (convergence case — see caveats)
If both businesses execute near plan and profits route through the waterfall:
~$107k net worth added in Year 1 → ~$2.2M by Year 10, with your hours *falling* from ~7/wk to ~3/wk
(`05-finance/consolidated-projection.csv`). **Downside case** (one business fails, weak markets,

even if a business doesn't.
