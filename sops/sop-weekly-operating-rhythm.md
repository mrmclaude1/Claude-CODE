# SOP — Weekly Operating Rhythm

> The fixed cadence that keeps the whole portfolio advancing on ≤5 hrs/week. Steady-state ≈ 4 hrs.

---

## The week at a glance

| Block | When | Time | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline** | Monday | 60 min | Leads & sales |
| **Delivery** | Wednesday | 90 min | Paying-client work (shrinks as you automate) |
| **Improve & Invest** | Friday | 60 min | KPIs, improvement loop, monthly investment |
| **Async approvals** | Throughout | ~30 min | Approve AI agent drafts |

---

## Monday — Pipeline (60 min)
1. Open the CRM. Review new leads and replies. (10)
2. Review the Outreach agent's drafted messages; edit and send the approved batch. (20)
3. Advance every open deal one step (book call, send proposal, follow up). (20)
4. Log pipeline value + reply rate to the dashboard. (10)

## Wednesday — Delivery (90 min)
1. Review the Pay-app/Waiver agent's drafted packages in your QA queue. (40)
2. **QA every money field and waiver form.** Approve or send back. (30)
3. Handle any REVIEW-flagged support replies. (10)
4. Log hours/client + QA error rate. (10)

> This block is the one automation is designed to shrink. As hours/client falls, redirect the freed time to growth — or to nothing (that's the point).

## Friday — Improve & Invest (60 min)
1. Read the Reporting agent's weekly scorecard; set statuses. (10)
2. Read the Research agent's digest; queue any HIGH actions. (10)
3. **Monthly weeks only:** run the 45-min improvement ritual (`docs/05`). Other weeks: skip to step 4.
4. Confirm the monthly automatic investment fired; apply the waterfall to new profit. (10)
5. Pick next week's single most important objective. (5)

## Async — Approvals (~30 min, spread out)
- Approve outreach, support, and content drafts as they appear. One-click for HIGH-confidence; edit for REVIEW.

---

## Rules
- **Hard cap: 10 hrs/week, target 5.** If you're over for two weeks running, something needs automating — that's a signal, not a normal state.
- **Protect the blocks.** Three short, scheduled blocks beat reactive all-day context-switching.
- **If a task recurs here weekly, write it an SOP. If an SOP runs >2×/month, automate it.**
