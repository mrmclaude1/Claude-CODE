# SOP — Sales Pipeline

> How a name becomes a paying client. Construction is a trust sale — this process respects that.

---

## Stages

| Stage | Definition | Exit criterion | Typical time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Sourced** | On the target list (network, AGC/ABC/CFMA roster) | First message sent | — |
| **2. Contacted** | First-touch sent | Reply received | follow-ups: 2–3 |
| **3. Discovery** | Call booked/held | Pain quantified (hrs/cycle, days-to-pay, current tool) | 1–2 wks |
| **4. Offer** | Soft offer made ("I'll do this for $X/mo") | Verbal interest | days |
| **5. Proposal** | `templates/proposal-template.md` sent | Terms agreed | 1–2 wks |
| **6. Closed-Won** | Agreement signed, first invoice paid | → Onboarding SOP | — |
| **Lost / Nurture** | No fit now | Reason logged; revisit in 90 days | — |

## Rules of the pipeline
- **Discovery before pitch.** Never propose before you've quantified their pain on a call. The number they say (hours, days late) becomes your ROI pitch and your case study.
- **One next step always scheduled.** No deal sits without a defined next action + date.
- **Follow up at least 3×.** ~42% of replies come from follow-ups; most competitors send one and quit.
- **Lead with outcome, not software.** "Get paid ~2 weeks faster / get your weekend back," not "AI-powered platform."
- **Ask every won client for one referral.** Cheapest CAC channel in the data; offer a free month for an intro that closes.
- **Log the reason for every loss.** Patterns in losses are product/positioning gold for the improvement loop.

## Weekly pipeline hygiene (Monday block)
1. Every deal advanced one stage or has a dated next step.
2. Stale deals (>14 days no movement) → re-engage or move to Nurture.
3. Pipeline value ≥ 3× monthly revenue target; if not, increase top-of-funnel outreach.

## Targets (early)
- 4+ discovery calls/week during validation.
- Reply rate ≥ 5% (personalize harder if below).
- Discovery → Closed-Won ≥ 20% (sub-first, warm network should beat cold B2B benchmarks).
